How We Work

From first sketch to final seal

Four steps, the same for every invitation suite, gift order or decor install — so you always know what happens next.

01

Consult

We learn your dates, your palette and the feeling you want guests to walk into.

02

Concept

Mood boards, paper swatches and a first design draft, refined with you until it's right.

03

Craft

Printing, foiling, packing and building — every piece assembled by hand in-studio.

04

Celebrate

Delivered or installed ahead of your date, so all that's left for you is to enjoy it.

01

Consult — Week 1

A short call or studio visit to talk through your event: guest count, dates, colours you're drawn to, and anything you've already seen and loved. We leave with a clear brief and a rough budget range.

02

Concept — Weeks 2–3

We put together a mood board and paper swatches, then a first design draft of your invitation or gift packaging. You get up to three rounds of revisions until every line feels right.

03

Craft — Weeks 4–6

Once approved, we move into production: printing, foiling, hand-assembly and quality checks. For decor, this is also when we source materials and confirm installation logistics.

04

Celebrate — Ahead of your date

Finished pieces are packed and delivered, or installed on-site, with buffer time built in before your event — so nothing is a last-minute scramble.
