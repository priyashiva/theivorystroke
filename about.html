The Studio

Paper, ink and a little bit of ceremony

The Ivory Stroke is a small studio designing custom invitations, return gifts and event decor for weddings and milestones — one considered detail at a time.

Our Story

Started at a kitchen table, one wedding card at a time

The Ivory Stroke began with a single hand-lettered invitation for a friend's wedding. Word travelled faster than we expected, and soon we were designing full suites, then favour boxes to match, then the welcome arch guests walked through. Today we work across invitations, return gifts and decor — but the studio still runs the way it started: small team, close attention, everything finished by hand.

Every detail, considered

"Our name comes from the ivory cardstock we fell for early on, and the single confident stroke of a calligraphy pen — the idea that one good line can carry an entire story."
